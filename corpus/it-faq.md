# Brightwell Health IT FAQ

Quick answers to the questions the Service Desk hears most often. Everything here is fictional and written for the Chunkwise demo.

## Accounts and Access

**Q: How do I reset my password?**
A: Go to the self-service portal at reset.brightwell.example and verify your identity with the authenticator app. Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and cannot reuse any of your previous ten passwords.

**Q: What happens after several failed sign-in attempts?**
A: Accounts lock automatically following five failed sign-ins. Wait fifteen minutes, or ring the Service Desk on extension 4357 for an immediate unlock.

**Q: How often do I need to change my password?**
A: You do not need to change it on a schedule. Passwords only need changing if you suspect they have been exposed, because forced periodic changes tend to produce weaker passwords.

**Q: Can I use multi-factor authentication without a smartphone?**
A: Yes. The Service Desk can issue a hardware security key, which plugs into a USB port and replaces the authenticator app.

## Devices

**Q: How do I request a new monitor or keyboard?**
A: Submit the Equipment Request form on the intranet. Standard accessories are shipped within five working days, and your manager is notified automatically.

**Q: My laptop is more than three years old. Can I get a new one?**
A: Laptops are replaced on a four-year cycle. If yours is failing earlier than that, raise a ticket and the Service Desk will assess whether a repair or a replacement makes more sense.

**Q: What should I do if my laptop is lost or stolen?**
A: Report it to the Service Desk immediately, day or night. The device will be remotely wiped, and you will need to file a police report within 48 hours if it was stolen.

**Q: Can I use my personal phone for work email?**
A: Yes, once you install the company's device management profile. The profile only controls the work container on your phone and cannot see your personal photos, messages or browsing history.

## Network and Remote Access

**Q: Do I need the VPN to use company applications?**
A: Most applications are available directly over the internet with single sign-on. The VPN is only needed for legacy finance systems and for administrators working on production infrastructure.

**Q: The office Wi-Fi is slow. Who should I tell?**
A: Post in the #it-help channel with your office location and the time the problem started. The network team monitors the channel during business hours.

**Q: Can I connect to public Wi-Fi in a café?**
A: Yes. All company applications use encrypted connections, so public Wi-Fi is safe for everyday work. Avoid handling patient data in public places where your screen can be seen.

## Software

**Q: How do I install software that is not in the Company Portal?**
A: Raise a software request ticket. The security team reviews new software within three working days, and approved applications are added to the Company Portal for everyone.

**Q: Which video-conferencing tool should I use?**
A: Use Brightwell Meet for all internal and external calls. Other tools may be used when a client insists, but recordings must never be stored outside company storage.

**Q: Is there an approved password manager?**
A: Yes, the company provides a password manager licence to every employee. Personal accounts on the same product are kept separate from your work vault.

## Security

**Q: I received a suspicious email. What should I do?**
A: Do not click any links or open attachments. Use the Report Phishing button in your mail client, which sends the message to the security team and removes it from your inbox.

**Q: I clicked a link in a phishing email by mistake. Am I in trouble?**
A: No. Report it to the security team straight away so they can check your account. Fast reporting limits the damage, and nobody is disciplined for an honest mistake that they report promptly.

**Q: How do I encrypt a file before sending it to a partner?**
A: Use the Secure Share feature in company storage instead of attaching the file to email. Secure Share links expire after seven days and record who opened the file.
