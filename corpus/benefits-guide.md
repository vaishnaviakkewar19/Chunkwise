# Brightwell Health Benefits Guide

This guide summarises the health, wellbeing and financial benefits available to Brightwell Health employees for the current plan year. All plans, prices and providers in this document are fictional and exist only to demonstrate document chunking.

Open enrolment runs from 1 to 30 November each year. Outside that window you can only change plans after a qualifying life event, such as marriage, the birth of a child, or losing coverage through a partner's employer. Qualifying changes must be reported within 31 days of the event.

## Medical Plans

Brightwell Health offers three medical plan tiers through our fictional carrier, Meridian Mutual. All tiers use the same national provider network; they differ in how costs are shared between you and the plan.

| Plan tier | Employee monthly premium | Annual deductible | Out-of-pocket maximum | Coinsurance |
|-----------|--------------------------|-------------------|-----------------------|-------------|
| Bronze    | $0                       | $3,000            | $7,000                | 30%         |
| Silver    | $45                      | $1,500            | $4,500                | 20%         |
| Gold      | $120                     | $500              | $2,500                | 10%         |

Premiums shown are for employee-only coverage. Adding a spouse or partner doubles the premium, and adding children adds a flat $60 per month regardless of how many children are covered.

### Copays

Copays are fixed amounts you pay at the time of service. They apply from the first visit and do not count towards your deductible, but they do count towards your out-of-pocket maximum.

| Service                    | Bronze | Silver | Gold |
|----------------------------|--------|--------|------|
| Primary care visit         | $40    | $25    | $10  |
| Specialist visit           | $80    | $50    | $30  |
| Urgent care                | $100   | $75    | $40  |
| Emergency room             | $400   | $250   | $150 |
| Virtual visit              | $0     | $0     | $0   |
| Generic prescription       | $15    | $10    | $5   |
| Brand-name prescription    | $60    | $40    | $25  |

Virtual visits are free on every tier because Brightwell Health wants employees to seek advice early rather than wait until a minor problem becomes a serious one.

### Choosing a Tier

If you expect to use healthcare only occasionally, Bronze usually has the lowest total annual cost. Employees who manage a chronic condition, take regular brand-name medication, or are planning a pregnancy often save money on Gold despite the higher premium. The benefits portal includes a cost estimator that compares your likely total spend under each tier.

## Dental

Dental coverage is provided by Brightsmile Dental, a fictional insurer, and is included at no cost for employees and their dependants.

| Treatment type       | Plan pays | Annual limit applies |
|----------------------|-----------|----------------------|
| Preventive (cleanings, X-rays) | 100% | No            |
| Basic (fillings, extractions)  | 80%  | Yes           |
| Major (crowns, bridges)        | 50%  | Yes           |
| Orthodontics (under 19 only)   | 50%  | Separate $2,000 lifetime limit |

The annual limit for basic and major treatment is $1,750 per covered person. Two preventive check-ups per year are covered in full and do not reduce the annual limit.

## Vision

Vision coverage includes one eye examination every 12 months with a $10 copay. In addition, the plan provides an allowance of $200 every 24 months towards glasses frames and lenses, or $180 towards contact lenses. Laser eye surgery is not covered, but members receive a 15% discount at participating clinics.

## Mental Health and Wellbeing

### Employee Assistance Programme

The Employee Assistance Programme, run by the fictional provider ClearPath, offers free, confidential support 24 hours a day. Employees and members of their household can access up to eight counselling sessions per issue per year, either by video or in person. ClearPath also provides legal and financial guidance by phone.

Brightwell Health never receives the names of people who use the programme. The company only sees anonymised monthly totals, which help us decide whether to expand the service.

### Wellbeing Allowance

Each employee receives a wellbeing allowance of $40 per month, which can be spent on gym memberships, fitness classes, meditation apps or sports equipment. Unused allowance accumulates within the calendar year but is reset to zero on 1 January.

## Financial Benefits

### Retirement Plan

Brightwell Health matches employee contributions to the retirement plan dollar-for-dollar up to 5% of base salary. Contributions above 5% are welcome but are not matched. The company match vests immediately, so it belongs to you from the first paycheque.

### Life and Disability Insurance

Life insurance equal to two times your annual base salary is provided at no cost. You may purchase additional cover of up to five times your salary through payroll deductions. Long-term disability insurance replaces 60% of base salary after a 90-day waiting period and continues until you are able to return to work or reach retirement age.

### Commuter Benefit

Employees who commute to an office can set aside up to $300 per month before tax for public transport passes or workplace parking. Elections can be changed monthly through the benefits portal and take effect from the first day of the following month.

## Getting Help

Questions about benefits should be sent to the Benefits Desk through the HR portal. The desk aims to reply within two working days. For urgent questions about medical coverage, for example while you are at a pharmacy or clinic, call the Meridian Mutual member line printed on the back of your insurance card.
