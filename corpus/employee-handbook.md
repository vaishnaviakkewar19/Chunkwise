# Brightwell Health Employee Handbook

Welcome to Brightwell Health. This handbook describes how we work together, what you can expect from the company, and what the company expects from you. Brightwell Health is a fictional organisation created for the Chunkwise demo; none of the policies below describe a real employer.

The handbook is reviewed every January by the People Team. When a policy changes mid-year, the updated wording is published on the intranet and announced in the monthly all-hands meeting. If anything in this handbook conflicts with your employment contract, the contract takes precedence.

## Our Values

Brightwell Health exists to make routine care feel effortless for patients and clinicians alike. Four values guide every decision we make, from hiring to product design.

### Patients First

Every project begins with the question "how does this help a patient?" Teams are encouraged to shadow clinic staff at least once per year so that the people building our tools understand the people using them. Shadowing days are paid and do not count against your leave balance.

### Default to Openness

We share information widely unless there is a specific reason not to. Meeting notes, roadmaps and incident reviews are published to the intranet by default. Confidential material, such as compensation data or patient information, is the exception and is always clearly labelled.

### Own the Outcome

When you pick up a piece of work, you are responsible for seeing it through or explicitly handing it over. Saying "I can no longer take this on" is always acceptable; letting work silently stall is not.

### Leave It Better

We expect everyone to improve the systems they touch, whether that is a process document, a codebase or the office kitchen. Small, continuous improvements matter more than occasional heroics.

## Working Hours and Location

### Core Hours

Our core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in your local time zone, Monday to Friday. Outside those hours you may structure your day as you see fit, provided your team agrees on how to reach you for urgent matters. Meetings should not be scheduled outside core hours without the attendee's consent.

### Hybrid Working

Most roles at Brightwell Health are hybrid. Employees are expected to spend at least two days per fortnight in one of our offices, ideally on the days their team has agreed to meet in person. Roles that involve hardware, clinical partners or facilities management may have different expectations, which your manager will explain during onboarding.

### Remote Work Stipend

This policy provides a monthly payment of $75 to every employee who works from home at least one day per week. It is paid automatically through payroll and is intended to cover home internet, electricity and minor office supplies. The payment is taxable and does not need to be claimed or receipted.

Large items such as desks, chairs and monitors are not covered by this stipend. They are ordered through the Equipment Request form described in the IT section of the intranet.

### Working Abroad

You may work from another country for up to 20 working days in any rolling twelve-month period. Requests must be submitted to the People Team at least three weeks in advance so that tax and data-protection implications can be checked. Working from a country under international sanctions is never permitted, and some client contracts forbid access to patient data from outside the home country altogether.

## Leave and Time Off

### Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in addition to public holidays. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata allowance based on their contracted hours. Leave accrues monthly, so a new starter who joins in July will have earned roughly half the annual allowance by the end of December.

Up to five unused days may be carried over into the following year. Carried-over days must be used by the end of March, after which they expire. Exceptions are only granted when leave was cancelled at the company's request, and must be approved in writing by a director.

### Sick Leave

If you are unwell, tell your manager as early as possible on the first day of absence, ideally before your normal start time. A message in your team's chat channel is acceptable for short absences. For absences longer than five consecutive working days, a note from a doctor or other qualified clinician is required.

Brightwell Health pays full salary for up to 20 days of sick leave in any rolling twelve-month period. Beyond that, statutory sick pay applies, although the People Team will always review long-term illness on a case-by-case basis and may extend full pay where appropriate.

### Parental Leave

Brightwell Health offers 18 weeks of fully paid parental leave to every new parent, regardless of gender or of whether the child joins the family through birth, adoption or surrogacy. The leave can be taken in a single block or split into up to three separate blocks.

To qualify for the full 18 weeks, you must have completed six months of continuous service by the expected date of birth or placement. Employees with shorter service still receive the leave, but the paid portion is reduced to six weeks, with the remainder available as unpaid leave.

Parents returning from leave may work a reduced schedule of three days per week at full pay for their first four weeks back. This phased return is optional and should be agreed with your manager before your leave begins.

### Bereavement Leave

Employees may take up to ten days of paid bereavement leave following the death of a close family member, and up to three days for the death of a friend or extended relative. No documentation is required. Managers are asked to approve bereavement leave immediately and to handle any follow-up conversations with care.

### Volunteering Days

Each employee may take two paid volunteering days per year to support a registered charity or community organisation. Volunteering days must be booked through the HR portal like annual leave, and the organisation's name should be entered in the comment field.

## Expenses and Travel

### Business Travel

Travel should be booked through the company travel portal so that our duty-of-care team knows where employees are. Economy class is the default for all flights. Premium economy may be booked for flights longer than six hours, and business class requires approval from a vice president.

Train travel is preferred over flying for any journey under four hours. Taxis and ride-hailing services may be used when public transport is impractical, for example late at night or when carrying equipment.

### Meals and Accommodation

When travelling on company business, you may spend up to $60 per day on meals, excluding alcohol. Hotel bookings should be in the standard room category and should not exceed the city cap published in the travel portal. Any amount above the cap is the employee's responsibility unless approved in advance.

### Submitting Expenses

Expenses must be submitted within 30 days of being incurred, using the Expenses app with a photo of each receipt attached. Claims submitted after 90 days will not be reimbursed. Approved expenses are paid with the next monthly payroll run.

## Learning and Development

### Learning Budget

Every employee has an annual learning budget of $1,500 that can be spent on courses, conferences, books or certifications related to their current role or a role they aspire to. Unused budget does not roll over. Requests above $500 require a short written justification that explains how the learning connects to your goals.

### Mentoring Programme

New employees are paired with a mentor from outside their reporting line during their first week. Mentors meet their mentees at least every two weeks for the first three months. After that, the pair decides together whether to continue, and either party may end the arrangement at any time without explanation.

## Conduct and Security

### Acceptable Use of Systems

Company laptops, accounts and software are provided for business purposes. Reasonable personal use, such as checking personal email during a break, is allowed. Installing unapproved software, disabling security tools, or sharing your credentials with anyone, including colleagues, is a serious breach of policy.

### Handling Patient Data

Patient data may only be accessed when it is needed to complete an assigned task. Every access is logged and audited monthly. Copying patient data to personal devices, personal cloud storage or email accounts is strictly prohibited, and doing so may result in dismissal and a report to the relevant regulator.

If you believe patient data has been exposed, report it to the Privacy Office within one hour of discovery, even if you are unsure. Speed matters more than certainty: the Privacy Office would rather investigate ten false alarms than miss one genuine incident.

### Raising Concerns

If you witness behaviour that conflicts with our values or the law, you can raise it with your manager, the People Team, or anonymously through the Speak Up line. Brightwell Health does not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Leaving Brightwell Health

### Notice Periods

The standard notice period is four weeks for individual contributors and eight weeks for people managers and directors. During the notice period you will be asked to document your current work and hand over open tasks to a named colleague.

### Returning Equipment

All company equipment must be returned on or before your final working day. A prepaid courier box will be sent to remote employees. Equipment that is not returned within 14 days of your departure will be deducted from your final pay at its current book value.
